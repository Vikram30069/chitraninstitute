Systematic Approach

From First Pencil Grip to Master Portrait Artist

Right from the basics of pencil-holding ergonomics and pressure control, to making them an accomplished portrait-sketching artist and painter, we have it all covered — through a systematic, detailed curriculum meticulously crafted by National Award-winning mentors and delivered by passionate gurus in the best possible way!

1

Ergonomic Pencil-Holding & Line Strokes

Dynamic tripod grip, stroke relaxation, pressure gradient calibration, and freehand line control.

2

Perspective, Proportion & Cast Shadows

1-point & 2-point perspective, negative space sighting, and 2B-8B graphite tonal shading dynamics.

3

Chromatic Color Mastery & Canvas Glazing

Soft pastels, transparent watercolor washes, and acrylic impasto sculpting with palette knives.

4

Portrait Realism, Tanjore 22K Gold & IFAA Diploma

Facial anatomy, European oil glazing, sacred 22K gold foil gilding, and solo gallery exhibition curation.
