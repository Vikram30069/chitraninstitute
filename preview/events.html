Annual Saraswati Puja Mega Cultural Day & Student Kalotsav

Every single year, Chitran Institute conducts its grand flagship Annual Event on the auspicious occasion of Saraswati Puja in February! It is an extraordinary, full-day celebration uniting over 300+ active participants—including energetic student performers, proud parents, dedicated faculty, art connoisseurs, and visiting dignitaries from across Hyderabad.

Last year, Chitran Institute had the great honor and privilege of inviting distinguished theatre maestro, playwright, and acclaimed actor Vinay Verma as our Chief Guest!
