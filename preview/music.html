PROGRESSIVE 6-TIER SYLLABUS

Electronic Keyboard & Western Piano Pathway

Under Vikram Sir (14+ years legacy, 100% Trinity pass record), keyboard learning is systematically graduated from foundational Indian melodies and rhythm synchronization, to cinematic film songs, Carnatic classical Varnams, Western Grand Staff notation, music theory, and prestigious Trinity London Diploma credentials.

🇬🇧 Trinity College London Grades 1–8 🇮🇳 Prayag Sangeet Samiti 🎼 Western Staff & Indian Swara Dual-Literacy
PART 1: INDIAN FUNDAMENTALS & RHYTHMS

Swara Geography, Indian Thaats & Rhythm Sync

  • Mapping the 7 natural Swaras (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni) across white and black keys.
  • Bilawal, Kalyan, Bhairav, and Khamaj thaat scales with proper right-hand fingering.
  • Metronome tempo alignment with Indian rhythmic cycles (Keherwa 8-beat, Dadra 6-beat, Teentaal 16-beat).
  • Single-finger and multi-finger Indian melody phrasing with steady rhythmic pulse.
Milestone Achievement: Play 5 devotional bhajans, patriotic tunes, and traditional melodies with steady metronomic rhythm.
PART 2: MOVIE SONGS & POPULAR MELODIES

Cinematic Melodies, Ear Playing & Left-Hand Chords

  • Decoding popular Bollywood, Tollywood, and evergreen melodies by ear and notation.
  • Left-hand harmony accompaniment: Major, Minor, Dominant 7th, and suspended triads.
  • Song anatomy: prelude intros, signature hook melodies, background score (BGM) interludes, and outros.
  • Dual-hand synchronization: melody on the right hand, steady chord comping on the left hand.
Milestone Achievement: Perform 4 complete cinematic songs with live chord transitions, intros, and rhythmic fills.
PART 3: CARNATIC CLASSICAL ON KEYBOARD

Varisais, Geethams, Varnams & Gamaka Articulation

  • Classical exercises: Sarali Varisai, Janta Varisai, and Dhatu Varisai across keys.
  • Alankaras in Sapta Taalas (Dhruva, Matya, Roopaka, Jhampa, Triputa, Ata, Eka).
  • Foundational Geethams: Sri Gananatha (Malahari), Kereya Neeranu, and Vara Veena (Mohanam).
  • Swarajathis and Adi Tala Varnams (Ninnukori in Mohanam, Jalajaksha in Hamsadhwani).
  • Emulating microtonal Gamakas through touch sensitivity, modulation, and keyboard pitch-wheel bends.
Milestone Achievement: Master 3 complete Geethams and 1 Adi Tala Varnam in two speeds (Prathama & Dvitiya Kala).
PART 4: WESTERN CLASSICAL, PIECES & SHEET MUSIC

Grand Stave Sight-Reading & Classical Conservatory Repertoire

  • Simultaneous reading of Treble Clef (melody) and Bass Clef (harmony) on the Grand Stave.
  • Classical repertoire pieces & etudes by J.S. Bach, W.A. Mozart, Beethoven, Clementi, and Burgmüller.
  • Dynamic articulation: Staccato, Legato, Slurs, Pianissimo (pp) to Fortissimo (ff), and Crescendo.
  • Key signatures (up to 4 sharps/flats), ledger lines, accidentals, and rhythmic time signatures (3/4, 4/4, 6/8).
Milestone Achievement: Sight-read an unseen 8-bar two-hand piece and perform 2 classical conservatory repertoire pieces from memory.
PART 5: MUSIC THEORY & ORIGINAL COMPOSITION

Harmonic Architecture, Scales, Arpeggios & Progressions

  • Scales across all 12 keys: Major, Natural/Harmonic/Melodic Minor, Pentatonic, and Blues scales.
  • Harmonic theory: Circle of Fifths, intervals, triads, inversions, and cadences (V-I, IV-I, ii-V-I).
  • Two-octave arpeggios, contrary motion scales, broken chords, and voice leading.
  • Original melodic composition, lead-sheet interpretation, and chord progression improvisation.
Milestone Achievement: Pass Grade 3–5 Western Music Theory assessment and compose an original 16-bar melodic theme.
PART 6: DIPLOMA & CERTIFICATION TRACK

Trinity College London Grade 1–8 & Associate Diploma (ATCL)

  • Trinity College London Electronic Keyboard & Western Piano Grade 1 to Grade 8 syllabus.
  • Technical exam requirements: scales, arpeggios, broken chords, and studies performed at strict exam tempos.
  • Comprehensive Aural Tests training: interval recognition, pulse tapping, cadence identification, and echo playback.
  • Associate Diploma (ATCL / DipABRSM / Sangeet Visharad) 30-minute concert recital preparation.
Milestone Achievement: Earn accredited Trinity College London Grade Certificate or Associate Diploma with 100% pass track record.

PERCUSSION MASTERY TRACK

Western Drums & Roland Octapad Pathway

Conjugated syllabus covering fundamental stick grip and rudiments, classic Western rock/pop/funk beats, intricate Jazz swing and brush work, energetic Indian cinematic rhythms (Bhangra, Dandiya, Tollywood), digital Roland Octapad sampling, and RockSchool/Trinity Grade 8 diplomas.

🇬🇧 Trinity & RockSchool Drum Kit Exam Syllabus ⚡ Roland SPD-20 / SPD-30 Pro Sampling Lab 🥁 4-Way Limb Independence & Meter Math
PART 1: RHYTHM FOUNDATION & RUDIMENT MECHANICS

Grip Ergonomics, 40 PAS Rudiments & Metronome Cadence

  • Matched grip and traditional grip biomechanics, wrist rebound physics, and fulcrum control.
  • Rhythmic counting and subdivisions: quarter notes, 8th notes, 16th notes, and 8th-note triplets.
  • Essential 40 PAS rudiments: Single Stroke Roll, Double Stroke Roll, Paradiddles, Flams, and Drags.
  • Dynamic control drills: accent vs tap strokes, ghost notes, and metronomic pocket stability.
Milestone Achievement: Execute clean single/double stroke rolls and paradiddles at 100 BPM with crisp dynamic control.
PART 2: WESTERN BEATS & CONTEMPORARY GROOVES

Pop, Rock, Funk 16-Beats & 4-Limb Independence

  • Standard 4/4 Pop & Rock grooves, driving straight-8ths, disco four-on-the-floor, and shuffle beats.
  • 16-beat funk grooves, syncopated kick drum patterns, and snappy snare backbeats on 2 and 4.
  • 4-limb independence: locking steady hi-hat foot chicks while playing independent kick and snare grooves.
  • Fill-in mechanics: 1-bar and 2-bar snare-to-tom fills, crash cymbal accentuation, and verse-chorus transitions.
Milestone Achievement: Perform 3 complete Western pop/rock tracks with studio-calibrated backing tracks and steady timekeeping.
PART 3: JAZZ DRUMMING & SWING REPERTOIRE

Jazz Swing Feel, Ride Cymbal Spang-a-Lang & Brush Work

  • The authentic Jazz swing feel, triplet ride cymbal pattern ("spang-a-lang"), and hi-hat chick on beats 2 & 4.
  • Feathering the bass drum softly, dynamic snare comping, polyrhythms, and rhythmic syncopation.
  • Wire brush techniques: lateral sweeping, circular stirring of the snare head, and ballad accompaniment.
  • Odd meters and time signatures: 3/4 Jazz Waltz, 5/4 swing, 6/8 Afro-Cuban, and 7/8 progressive meters.
Milestone Achievement: Play a 32-bar jazz standard with ride cymbal swing, brush work, and interactive snare/kick comping.
PART 4: INDIAN RHYTHMS & CINEMATIC GROOVES

Bollywood, Tollywood, Bhangra Dhol Beats & Fusion

  • Adapting Indian rhythmic patterns onto Western drum kits for Bollywood and Tollywood soundtrack songs.
  • Bhangra 12/8 Dhol grooves translated to kick and snare syncopation with dynamic cymbal splashes.
  • Energetic Dandiya/Garba rhythmic variations, South Indian Kuthu beats, and fast celebratory folk grooves.
  • Translating Indian Tala cycles (Keherwa, Dadra, Roopak 7/8, Jhaptal 10/8) into fusion drumming frameworks.
Milestone Achievement: Perform a live 3-song Indian cinematic medley spanning romantic 6/8 ballads to high-speed 12/8 folk grooves.
PART 5: ROLAND OCTAPAD (SPD-20 / SPD-30)

Electronic Percussion Architecture, Patch Editing & Stage Sampling

  • Hardware architecture: pad sensitivity calibration, velocity curves, dual-trigger assignment, and threshold tuning.
  • Authentic Indian percussion programming: Tabla, Dholak, Mridangam, Ghatam, Kanjira, Duff, Dhol, and Chanda patches.
  • Western electronic kits, 808 trap kits, Latin percussion (Congas, Bongos, Timbales), and layered synth textures.
  • Real-time phrase looping, external foot-pedal kick/hi-hat integration, and live stage accompaniment.
Milestone Achievement: Program a custom multi-instrument percussion patch and perform live accompaniment using the Roland Octapad.
PART 6: GRADED EXAMS & DRUM KIT DIPLOMA

Trinity College London & RockSchool Drum Kit Grade 1–8

  • Complete preparation for Trinity College London and RockSchool Drum Kit Grade 1 to Grade 8 practical exams.
  • Drum chart score reading: deciphering repeat signs, codas, slash notation, drum key maps, and fills.
  • Quick study sight-reading tests, ear training playback, and technical rudiment exam performance.
  • Solo drum performance, advanced polyrhythmic coordination, and international Associate Diploma certification.
Milestone Achievement: Clear Trinity College London / RockSchool Drum Kit Graded Exams with Distinction honors.

INDIAN CLASSICAL TRADITION

Classical Indian Tabla & Percussion Pathway

Mentored by Suresh Sir (All India Radio Graded Percussionist), covering pure Bols and hand techniques (nikas), foundational and intricate Taals (Teentaal, Keherwa, Dadra, Roopak, Jhaptal), Kaidas, Paltas, Tihais, vocal/instrumental Sangat, and Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Visharad diplomas.

🇮🇳 Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya 📜 Prayag Sangeet Samiti Visharad Diploma 🪘 Delhi & Banaras Classical Gharanas
PART 1: FUNDAMENTAL BOLS & HAND TECHNIQUE (NIKAS)

Dayan & Bayan Nikas, Syllable Clarity & Sound Purity

  • Dayan (treble drum): Ta, Tin, Te, Te-Te, Tun, Na, and Ti produced from chaati, sur, and maidan zones.
  • Bayan / Dagga (bass drum): Ge, Ghe, Ka, Ke, and Kat with controlled wrist pressure and bass modulations (meend).
  • Combined compound bols: Dha (Ta + Ge), Dhin (Tin + Ghe) with balanced resonance.
  • Ergonomic seating posture, hand arch, finger discipline, and resonance clarity with zero unwanted buzzing.
Milestone Achievement: Produce all fundamental bols with pure acoustic clarity and execute smooth meend bass glides on the Dagga.
PART 2: ESSENTIAL RHYTHMIC CYCLES (TAALS & THEKAS)

Teentaal, Keherwa, Dadra, Roopak, Jhaptal & Ektaal

  • Teentaal (16 matras: 4 vibhags — Dha Dhin Dhin Dha | Dha Dhin Dhin Dha | Dha Tin Tin Ta | Ta Dhin Dhin Dha).
  • Keherwa (8 matras: Dha Ge Na Ti | Na Ke Dhi Na) and Dadra (6 matras: Dha Dhi Na | Dha Ti Na).
  • Roopak (7 matras: Tin Tin Na | Dhin Na | Dhin Na — unique tala commencing on Khaali).
  • Jhaptal (10 matras: 2+3+2+3) and Ektaal (12 matras: 6 vibhags of 2).
  • Mastery of Sam, Taali (claps), and Khaali (waves) hand gestures while reciting the theka.
Milestone Achievement: Recite padhant and play Teentaal, Keherwa, Dadra, and Roopak at Vilambit and Madhya laya with accurate taali/khaali.
PART 3: CLASSICAL COMPOSITIONS & ELABORATIONS

Kaidas, Paltas, Relas, Tukras, Chakradars & Tihais

  • Kaidas: Delhi and Banaras gharana theme compositions with strict structural rules.
  • Paltas: Systematic mathematical permutations of syllables preserving the kaida's identity.
  • Tihais: Triple-repetition rhythmic phrases landing precisely on the Sam (Beat 1).
  • High-speed Relas (cascading rolls such as Dhere-Dhere), classical Tukras, and 3-cycle Chakradars.
Milestone Achievement: Deliver 2 complete Kaidas in Teentaal with 4 Paltas each, ending in a crisp Tihai and rapid Rela.
PART 4: ACCOMPANIMENT (SANGAT MASTERY)

Sangat for Vocal, Harmonium, Bhajans, Ghazals & Instruments

  • Vocal Sangat: accompanying Hindustani Classical Khayal, semi-classical Thumri, Dadra, Ghazals, and Bhajans.
  • Instrumental Sangat: accompanying Harmonium, Sitar, Violin, Flute, and Keyboard.
  • Maintaining rock-solid laya without wavering, framing the soloist, providing dynamic laggi flourishes, and matching tihais.
  • Acoustic tuning: tuning the Dayan using a brass hammer to match the soloist's keynote (Sa).
Milestone Achievement: Accompany a live vocalist and harmonium player through a complete 15-minute classical bhajan and thumri recital.
PART 5: LAYA & LAYAKARI MATHEMATICS

Vilambit, Madhya, Drut & Metric Layakari (Thah, Dugun, Tigun, Chaugun)

  • Mastery across tempo tiers: Vilambit (slow laya), Madhya (medium laya), and Drut (fast laya).
  • Layakari variations: Thah (1:1 single speed), Dugun (2:1 double), Tigun (3:1 triple), and Chaugun (4:1 quadruple).
  • Cross-rhythmic fractional complexities: Kuwad (5/4 speed) and Biwad (7/4 speed) rhythmic play.
  • Mathematical calculation of Bedam and Damdar Tihais starting from any arbitrary matra of the cycle.
Milestone Achievement: Recite padhant and perform Teentaal theka across Thah, Dugun, Tigun, and Chaugun with zero tempo drift.
PART 6: GANDHARVA & SAMITI VISHARAD DIPLOMA

Recognized Classical Diploma & Solo Concert Recital Track

  • Akhil Bharatiya Gandharva Mahavidyalaya Mandal & Prayag Sangeet Samiti curriculum.
  • Progressive levels: Prarambhik, Praveshika Pratham & Purna, Madhyama Pratham & Purna, and Visharad (6-Year Bachelor's equivalent).
  • Theoretical Viva: Gharana lineages (Delhi, Ajrara, Farrukhabad, Lucknow, Banaras, Punjab), Pandit Bhatkhande notation, and tala acoustics.
  • Solo concert recital before university examiners demonstrating comprehensive traditional mastery.
Milestone Achievement: Achieve government-recognized Sangeet Visharad Diploma in Tabla with First Class honors.

SRUTI & VOICE CULTURE

Carnatic Classical Vocal & Sanskrit Slokas Pathway

Mentored by Sumayukthi Ma'am, training students from foundational Abhyasa Ganam and sruti alignment to intricate Kritis, authentic Sanskrit sloka recitation, voice modulation, and university-certified vocal diplomas.

🎤 Sruti Shuddham & Microtonal Pitch 📜 Authentic Sanskrit Chanting Diction 🏛️ University & Samiti Diploma Certified
PART 1: ABHYASA GANAM FOUNDATIONS

Sruti Alignment, Sarali, Janta & Dhatu Varisais

  • Tambura drone synchronization and achieving pitch-perfect Sruti Shuddham.
  • Sarali Varisais (14 fundamental exercises in Mayamalavagowla raga) in 3 speeds.
  • Janta Varisais (twin-note stress drills) and Dhatu Varisais (zig-zag melodic permutations).
  • Mandrasthayi and Tarasthayi varisais extending vocal range across two octaves.
Milestone: Sing all Sarali & Janta Varisais in 3 speeds with unwavering sruti alignment.
PART 2: SAPTA TALA ALANKARAS & GEETHAMS

Tala Mathematics & Pillari Geethams in Multiple Ragas

  • Alankaras in 35 Tala scheme with focus on the core 7: Dhruva, Matya, Roopaka, Jhampa, Triputa, Ata, Eka.
  • Pillari Geethams: Sri Gananatha (Malahari), Kereya Neeranu, Padumanabha, and Kundagowra.
  • Advanced Geethams in Mohanam (Vara Veena) and Kalyani (Kamalajadala).
  • Swarajathis: Rara Venu (Bilahari) and Samba Siva (Khamas).
Milestone: Render 5 Geethams and 2 Swarajathis with pristine lyrical Sahitya diction.
PART 3: VARNAMS & TWO-SPEED MASTERY

Tana Varnams in Adi & Ata Talas across Classical Ragas

  • Ninnukori in Mohanam (Adi Tala) and Jalajaksha in Hamsadhwani.
  • Evari Bodha in Abhogi and Sami Ninne in Sriragam.
  • Singing Varnams in Prathama (single) and Dvitiya (double) kala with flawless tala tracking.
  • Gamakas: Kampita, Sphurita, Nokku, Ravai, and Odukkal voice nuances.
Milestone: Perform 3 complete Adi Tala Varnams in two speeds without missing a beat.
PART 4: KRITIS & TRINITY COMPOSITIONS

Master Compositions of Saint Tyagaraja, Dikshitar & Syama Sastri

  • Compositions: Vatapi Ganapatim (Hamsadhwani), Samajavaragamana (Hindolam), Brochevarevarura (Khamas).
  • Lyrical interpretation (Bhavam), microtonal embellishments, and Sangatis in Pallavi, Anupallavi, and Charanam.
  • Raga Lakshana identification and preliminary Raga Alapana phrases.
  • Concert presentation decorum, microphone technique, and accompanist coordination.
Milestone: Deliver 4 classical Kritis with expressive Bhava and nuanced sangatis.
PART 5: SANSKRIT SLOKAS & DEVOTIONAL STOTRAMS

Authentic Vedic Diction, Voice Culture & Stotram Chanting

  • Sri Vishnu Sahasranama Stotram and Sri Lalitha Sahasranama Stotram with pristine Sanskrit phonetics.
  • Bhagavad Gita select adhyayas (Chapters 2, 12, and 15) with traditional metre.
  • Devotional compositions: Annamacharya Sankeerthanas, Purandara Dasa Devaranamas, and Ramadasu Kirtanas.
  • Breath control (Pranayama), diaphragm resonance, and vocal cord preservation techniques.
Milestone: Recite complete Vishnu Sahasranamam with authentic Vedic intonation and clarity.
PART 6: UNIVERSITY VOCAL DIPLOMA CERTIFICATION

Potti Sreeramulu Telugu University & Gandharva Samiti Diploma

  • Structured curriculum adhering to state university and Gandharva Mahavidyalaya boards.
  • Certificate, Diploma, and Sangeet Visharad examination preparation.
  • Theoretical viva: Tala structures, Melakarta 72 raga classification, music history, and composer biographies.
  • Solo stage Arangetram / Kacheri concert performance with violin and mridangam accompaniment.
Milestone: Graduate with accredited University / Samiti Diploma in Carnatic Vocal.

CONTEMPORARY & CLASSICAL

Acoustic, Classical & Lead Guitar Pathway

Mentored by Vikram Sir & Kalyan Sir, guiding students through fingerboard ergonomics, open/barre chords, fingerstyle plucking, pentatonic scale solos, popular Bollywood/Western tracks, and Trinity College London / RockSchool graded exams.

🇬🇧 Trinity & RockSchool Guitar Grades 1–8 🎸 Fingerstyle Plucking & PIMA Technique 🎼 Lead Guitar Solos & Western Staff Tabs
PART 1: FRETBOARD ANATOMY & CHROMATICS

Posture, Tuning, 1-2-3-4 Finger Drills & Plectrum Control

  • Guitar anatomy, standard E-A-D-G-B-E tuning, and proper left-hand thumb placement behind the neck.
  • Chromatic spider exercises across all 6 strings building independent finger strength.
  • Alternate picking mechanics with plectrum, wrist economy, and string-skipping coordination.
  • Reading basic guitar tab notation and fretboard note mapping.
Milestone: Execute clean alternate picking chromatic drills at 90 BPM with zero fret buzz.
PART 2: OPEN CHORDS, BARRE CHORDS & STRUMMING

Chord Transitions, Rhythm Dynamics & Syncopated Strumming

  • Open chords: C, G, D, Em, Am, E, A, and Dm with smooth, instant transitions.
  • Conquering F Major and B Minor movable barre chords without hand fatigue.
  • Rhythmic strumming patterns in 4/4, 3/4, and 6/8: down-up dynamics, percussive chucking, and palm muting.
  • Accompanying popular Bollywood, Tollywood, and Western acoustic songs.
Milestone: Transition seamlessly between 8 open and barre chords while maintaining steady rhythm.
PART 3: FINGERSTYLE PLUCKING & ARPEGGIOS

PIMA Classical Technique, Travis Picking & Broken Chords

  • Classical right-hand finger designation: Pulgar (thumb), Indice (index), Medio (middle), Anular (ring).
  • Cascading arpeggio patterns: Giuliani right-hand studies and acoustic ballad fingerpicking.
  • Travis picking: alternating bass notes on low strings while plucking melody on high strings.
  • Performance of classical etudes (Carulli, Sor, Tarrega) and contemporary fingerstyle arrangements.
Milestone: Perform 2 complete fingerstyle instrumental arrangements with independent bass lines.
PART 4: LEAD SCALES, BLUES & IMPROVISATION

Pentatonic Boxes, Hammer-Ons, Pull-Offs & Expressive Bends

  • Major and Minor Pentatonic scale box shapes across the entire fretboard.
  • Blues scale, the "blue note", and improvising over 12-bar blues chord progressions.
  • Lead guitar articulation: hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, string bends, and finger vibrato.
  • Electric guitar pickup switching, amplifier gain staging, and tone shaping.
Milestone: Improvise an expressive 16-bar blues and rock guitar solo over a live backing track.
PART 5: CINEMATIC REPERTOIRE & ENSEMBLE JAM

Indian Film Themes, Western Classics & Band Dynamics

  • Acoustic adaptations of classic Ilaiyaraaja, A.R. Rahman, and Pritam film scores.
  • Western pop and rock acoustic standards (Hotel California, Tears in Heaven, Shape of You).
  • Jamming with percussionists and keyboardists in multi-instrument acoustic ensembles.
  • Live stage performance presence, capos, and acoustic pickup amplification.
Milestone: Lead the rhythm and lead acoustic guitars in a 3-song student band ensemble.
PART 6: TRINITY & ROCKSCHOOL GUITAR DIPLOMA

Trinity College London / RockSchool Grades 1–8 Certification

  • Trinity College London Acoustic / Classical Guitar Grade 1 to 8 examination syllabus.
  • RockSchool Contemporary Guitar examination track with backing tracks.
  • Technical work: two-octave scales, arpeggios, technical studies, and quick-study sight-reading.
  • Associate Diploma certification establishing professional performing and teaching credentials.
Milestone: Clear Trinity College London / RockSchool Guitar Grade Exam with Merit or Distinction.
