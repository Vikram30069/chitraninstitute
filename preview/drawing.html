COMPREHENSIVE PEDAGOGY

10+ Specialized Artistic Mediums Under One Roof

Whether you are a beginner exploring pencils or an advanced artist pursuing an exhibition-grade oil canvas, our curriculum nurtures your individual voice.

✏️

Pencil Sketching & Shading

Foundations of line weight, geometric structure, cross-hatching, light & cast shadow rendering using 2B to 8B graphite pencils, charcoal stumps, and still life setups.

Beginner to Advanced
🎨

Canvas Oil Painting Realism

Classical European glazing, underpainting, fat-over-lean layering, color temperature mixing, portrait rendering, and archival varnish preservation on stretched linen.

Classical Master Track
🌊

Watercolor Washes & Transparency

Wet-on-wet technique, wet-on-dry edge control, atmospheric perspective, gradient sky washes, botanical illustration, and dynamic landscape watercolors.

Pure Fluid Medium
🖌️

Acrylic on Canvas & Impasto

Fast-drying vibrant pigments, modern acrylic blending, heavy-body impasto textures, abstract expressionism, and contemporary realism on canvas boards.

Contemporary Expression
✨

Tanjore 22K Pure Gold Foil Art

Centuries-old South Indian traditional craft: sacred iconography, chalk-paste relief embossing (Mukadwara), pure 22-karat gold leaf gilding, and Jaipur gemstone studding.

Traditional Heritage
🔪

Palette Knife 3D Relief Painting

Sculptural knife work creating bold, three-dimensional peaks and textures. Learn architectural brickwork, ocean wave crests, and dynamic floral impasto.

Tactile Sculptural
🖤

Charcoal & Portrait Anatomy

Deep rich velvet blacks, vine and compressed charcoal, facial anatomy proportions, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, and lifelike expressive human portraiture.

Expressive Realism
🌈

Soft & Oil Pastels

Vibrant color layering, finger blending, sgraffito etching, tonal harmony, and velvety textured surfaces on toned archival papers.

Color Harmony
🏺

3D Glass & Terracotta Pot Painting

Multi-dimensional stained glass outline leads, glass color transparency, ceramic pot relief embossing, and decorative metallic paints.

Decorative Crafts
🧣

Fabric, Silk & Batik Art

Textile painting techniques, floral freehand motifs, cold-wax batik dyeing, and permanent fabric color fixation on cotton, silk, and canvas bags.

Textile Art
