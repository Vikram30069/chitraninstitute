INFRASTRUCTURE & FACILITIES

Designed for Mastery: A Tour of Our Studios

Every space at Chitran Institute has been thoughtfully engineered for acoustic clarity, optimal natural lighting, safety, and immersive creative focus.

FINE ART ATELIER

The Classical Drawing & Tanjore Atelier

Equipped with professional wooden easels, directional daylight windows, specialized canvas storage, and dedicated 22K gold foil Tanjore embossing stations. Mentored personally by Suryachandra Awardee Guru Rajkumar Banerjee.

  • ✓ Professional drafting easels & palettes
  • ✓ Tanjore 22K pure gold leaf workstations
  • ✓ Oil paint drying and archival canvas racks
INSTRUMENTAL SUITE

Acoustic Piano & Instrumental Sound Lab

Acoustically dampened studio featuring authentic Yamaha acoustic upright pianos, Roland touch-sensitive keyboards, Fender acoustic/lead guitars, digital drum pods, and an authentic classical Indian Tabla riyaz alcove.

  • ✓ Yamaha upright piano & Roland synthesizer stations
  • ✓ Sound-dampened acoustic wall panels
  • ✓ Individual headphone monitoring for focus
DANCE STUDIO

Mirror-Lined Dance Rehearsal Studio

Air-conditioned rehearsal studio featuring full-length practice mirrors, clear acoustics, and attentive instructor guidance for energetic Western hip-hop routines and disciplined classical Kuchipudi footwork.

  • ✓ Attentive instructor feedback in small batches
  • ✓ Full-length wall mirrors & dedicated footwork coaching
  • ✓ High-fidelity studio sound setup
PENMANSHIP POD

Ergonomic Handwriting & Abacus Suite

Custom-built drafting tables angled at 15 degrees for optimal spinal posture and effortless wrist movement. Equipped with calibrated lighting, calligraphy nib stations, and Soroban abacus mental math racks.

  • ✓ Ergonomic posture-correct drafting desks
  • ✓ 1-on-1 stroke diagnostic magnifying tools
  • ✓ Japanese Soroban Abacus speed boards
