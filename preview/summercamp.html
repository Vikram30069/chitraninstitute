FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Q: Is the Summer Camp held every year?

Yes! Chitran Institute has proudly conducted its flagship Multi-Activity Summer Camp every single year during school summer holidays (April and May) since 2003. Hundreds of children have started their artistic and musical journeys right here.

Q: What are the studio facilities and safety standards?

All drawing ateliers, instrumental music suites, dance practice studios, and waiting areas are well-equipped, safe, and comfortable with RO drinking water, air conditioning, and continuous faculty supervision, ensuring children learn and create with complete focus and joy.

Q: Are art materials provided by the institute?

Yes. All canvas boards, professional acrylic paints, brushes, palettes, terracotta clay, pastels, origami papers, and workbooks are provided directly by Chitran Institute. Children do not need to purchase heavy supplies.

Q: Can my child continue regular classes after summer camp?

Absolutely! Summer camp students receive guaranteed direct admission into our year-round accredited Diploma tracks in Drawing, Trinity Keyboard, Western Dance, or Handwriting with special alumni fee concessions.
