## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Do I need to own an instrument before joining?
For campus classes, we provide instruments during your class sessions. Having an instrument at home is recommended for daily practice, and our faculty can advise you on selecting the right beginner model.

#### What is the recommended age to start music?
Children aged 5.5+ can easily begin Keyboard, Piano, or Vocal training. Guitar and Drums are typically ideal for ages 7+. Adults of all ages can join anytime!

#### Can I prepare for international music certification exams?
Yes! Our keyboard and guitar faculty provide complete syllabus guidance for Trinity College London and RockSchool grade examinations.
