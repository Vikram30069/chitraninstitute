## 2. Specialized Art Disciplines Taught

### A. Drawing & Pencil Sketching (Foundations to Advanced)
- **What You Learn**: Line control, geometric structure, cross-hatching, light source & cast shadows, pencil shading (2B to 8B), charcoal rendering, portraiture, perspective drawing, landscape composition, and still life.
- **Ideal For**: Beginners (ages 4.5+), school students, architecture (NATA) aspirants, and Fine Arts entrance preparation.
- **Dedicated Page Link**: `[ Learn More about Drawing & Sketching → ]` (`/drawing-classes-hyderabad/`)

### B. Watercolor Painting (Transparency & Fluidity)
- **What You Learn**: Color theory, color mixing, wet-on-wet technique, wet-on-dry, dry brushing, gradient washes, masking, atmospheric perspective, botanical illustrations, and dynamic landscapes.
- **Dedicated Page Link**: `[ Learn More about Watercolor Classes → ]` (`/watercolor-painting-classes-hyderabad/`)

### C. Acrylic Painting & Knife Painting (Vibrant & Contemporary)
- **What You Learn**: Canvas preparation, layering, blending, impasto texture creation, palette knife techniques, modern abstract art, realism, and color harmony on stretched canvas boards.
- **Dedicated Page Link**: `[ Learn More about Acrylic Painting → ]` (`/acrylic-oil-painting-classes-hyderabad/`)

### D. Oil Painting (Classical Masters Technique)
- **What You Learn**: Fat-over-lean principle, underpainting, glazing, scumbling, tone mixing, portrait rendering, classical realism, and canvas preservation.
- **Dedicated Page Link**: `[ Learn More about Oil Painting → ]` (`/acrylic-oil-painting-classes-hyderabad/`)

### E. Traditional Indian & Decorative Art Forms
- **Disciplines**: 
  - **Tanjore Painting**: Traditional 22k gold foil embossing, Jaipur gemstone studding, and deity iconography.
  - **Fabric Painting**: Block print styling, freehand floral motifs on silk/cotton, color fixing.
  - **3D Glass & Pot Painting**: Multi-layer stained glass outlines, ceramic pot detailing, and terracotta embossing.
- **Dedicated Page Link**: `[ Learn More about Traditional Arts → ]` (`/traditional-art-classes-hyderabad/`)

### F. Fine Arts Diploma & Government Exam Coaching
- **Curriculum**: Formal syllabus covering theoretical art history, practical drawing, composition, portfolio curation, and mock examinations for state/national Fine Arts diplomas and BFA admissions.
- **Dedicated Page Link**: `[ Learn More about Diploma Prep → ]` (`/fine-arts-diploma-hyderabad/`)
